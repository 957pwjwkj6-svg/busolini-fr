Consulting

Three offers with a sheet to download, then advisory work for startups and boards. Terms are discussed in a first conversation.

For CISOs

CISO masterclass programme

Two full days, three to six months apart. The first, 100 Days to Credibility, secures the mandate. The second, Building Your Operating Model, designs the function that uses it and ends with a written twelve-month build sequence.

Each day runs 09:00 to 16:30, in person, for up to thirty participants. Participants keep ten documents across the two days. Day one was piloted in September 2026.

Programme prospectus (PDF)

Digital assets

Digital asset risk assessment

For firms and family offices that hold digital assets. An assessment of how the assets are actually run: who can move them, what an approver can verify on the device in front of them, how holdings reconcile to the chain, and what happens on death, incapacity or dispute.

You receive a rated risk register, a 30, 60 and 90-day roadmap, and an immediate attention notice if something cannot wait.

One-page sheet (PDF)

Executives and families

VIP digital life protection

Attacks on prominent people increasingly reach them through their family, their staff and their devices. The assessment treats each person around the principal at their own tier, and starts with the people and accounts that can move money or recover an account on the principal's word.

You receive a report for the principal and one for each person, a 30, 60 and 90-day roadmap, and step-by-step guides the family keeps.

Boards

Board advisory and membership

I put technical risk in front of a board as a funding decision rather than a briefing. I am seeking Risk, Audit or Technology committee roles in banking, financial services, fintech or digital infrastructure, in Europe, Switzerland or the UAE.

  • Functional reporting to the board risk committee of a Middle East bank, with direct access to the CEO.
  • Co-created that bank's AI and data governance committee and defined its AI risk management framework, mapped to the NIST AI RMF, covering more than 50 AI models.
  • Zero adverse audit findings across 11 consecutive years of internal audit and regulatory review at two banks, spanning FINMA, MAS and HKMA.
  • Advisory board member, Trust Valley Association, Geneva, since 2022.
