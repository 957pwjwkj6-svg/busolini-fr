Advisory

For CISOs

CISO strategy and roadmap

Advice to CISOs on defining a security strategy the board will fund, and on turning it into a roadmap that delivery teams can run. The work covers risk appetite, target operating model, sequencing and the case for funding.

  • Secured board and ExCo validation of a five-year, risk-based strategy at a bank in the Middle East: annual operating budget doubled to $20M, team scaled by more than 50% to 85 FTE.
  • Earlier, secured board and ExCo funding for a four-year, NIST CSF-aligned programme covering 30 security domains and about 800 deliverables.
  • Reduced supervisory audit findings from more than a dozen to a handful within 12 months.

Digital product security

Shift left and shift up

Security built into the product lifecycle of digital banks and digital companies. Shifting left puts requirements, design review and testing at the start of delivery. Shifting up enables the business to take more autonomous information security and privacy risk decisions, so that products reach the market faster within the risk appetite.

  • Designed a digital security operating model with business information security officers, digital security leads and security champions embedded in product squads.
  • Built AI-driven DevSecOps tooling and a secure CI/CD platform.
  • Embedded security by design in the product cycles of a regulated digital asset bank, with an auditable regulatory posture.

AI

AI risk governance and AI security

Advice on rolling out AI risk governance and AI security across a company: who decides, which models are in scope, how risk is rated, and how security teams test and monitor what the business deploys.

  • Defined a bank's AI risk management framework in 2024, mapped to CBUAE guidelines and the NIST AI RMF, covering more than 50 AI models.
  • Co-created its AI and data governance committee, with the CISO function as strategic co-owner.

Programmes and assessments

For CISOs

CISO masterclass programme

Two full days, three to six months apart. The first, 100 Days to Credibility, secures the mandate. The second, Building Your Operating Model, designs the function that uses it and ends with a written twelve-month build sequence.

Each day runs 09:00 to 16:30, in person, for up to thirty participants. Participants keep ten documents across the two days. Day one was piloted in September 2026.

Programme prospectus (PDF)

Digital assets

Digital asset risk assessment

For firms and family offices that hold digital assets. An assessment of how the assets are actually run: who can move them, what an approver can verify on the device in front of them, how holdings reconcile to the chain, and what happens on death, incapacity or dispute.

You receive a rated risk register, a 30, 60 and 90-day roadmap, and an immediate attention notice if something cannot wait.

One-page sheet (PDF)See the assessment in practice (PDF)

Executives and families

VIP digital life protection

Attacks on prominent people increasingly reach them through their family, their staff and their devices. The assessment treats each person around the principal at their own tier, and starts with the people and accounts that can move money or recover an account on the principal's word.

You receive a report for the principal and one for each person, a 30, 60 and 90-day roadmap, and step-by-step guides the family keeps.

Partnership

Senior expert teams, built per project.

Some mandates need more than one profile. For these, I build a virtual team of senior experts around the project and act as the single point of contact for the client. The team is not limited to cybersecurity: it draws on senior people in risk management, audit, regulatory engagement, technology and leadership, chosen for the question at hand and disbanded when the work ends.

  • One point of contact and one accountable lead.
  • A team composed per project.
  • Scope, team and terms agreed in a first conversation.

About

The short version of the career.

I am Global Head of Information Security (CISO) at a bank in the Middle East, reporting to the Chief Risk Officer, with direct access to the CEO and functional reporting to the board risk committee. I have spent more than 35 years in professional life and more than 25 in cybersecurity leadership, across Tier-1 banks in France, Switzerland and the Gulf, after an early career at the French Ministry of Defence and ANSSI, France's national cybersecurity agency.

I work from one thesis: cyber resilience is a competitive advantage, and security investment is a business enabler, not a cost centre. At my current bank I secured board validation of a five-year strategy that doubled annual operating expenditure to $20M and scaled the security organisation to 85 FTE. I also defined its AI risk management framework, which governs more than 50 AI models.

I have operated under four supervisory authorities and kept zero adverse audit findings across 11 consecutive years of review at two banks. Gartner selected my work as a case study in CISO role evolution (London, 2025), and the ISACA UAE Chapter gave me its CISO Infosec and Cyber Risk Leadership Award in 2024. I am working towards non-executive director roles.
